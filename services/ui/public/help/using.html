Using Keen

Keen connects the records your systems already produce with the controls, risks and audits you are accountable for. This handbook takes one topic at a time. Each chapter opens with what it helps you do, keeps the steps short, and folds the finer points into Going deeper panels you can open when you want them.

If you are new, read chapter 1 and then the chapter for the screen in front of you. You can finish any chapter on its own.

Choose a chapter, or search all handbooks by task, record type or configuration name.
