Administering Keen

This handbook is for the people who install Keen, decide who may use it, connect it to the systems it collects from, and keep it healthy. Each chapter is one job: deploy, configure sign-in, add a source, manage retention, recover. Settings are named exactly as they appear in the environment, and the finer points sit in Going deeper panels.

Two roles share the work. Application administrators maintain frameworks, features and evidence definitions inside Keen. Systems administrators operate the runtime, credentials, networking and backups; in a managed installation the hosting operator does that part.
