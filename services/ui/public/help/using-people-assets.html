People, suppliers and assets

Four registers describe who and what your management system covers: the organisation chart, people, vendors and assets, with an access matrix that records who should be able to reach what. This chapter builds them in the order that keeps their links valid.

Organisation chart, people, vendors and assets

Build the organisation chart

In ISMS → Organisation chart, create a node with a name, type, optional parent, users and description, and associate the relevant controls and clauses. Parents represent your hierarchy; zoom and reset help with larger charts. Keep roles and people distinct, so a personnel change does not erase what a role means.

Maintain the People directory

In People & vendors → People, choose Add person or open a name and select Edit person to record a name, optional e-mail and position, an optional Keen account and organisation role, the assets or devices used, and notes. A person does not need a login. Changing the account of someone who sits quizzes needs quiz.manage, because the account decides whose quiz rounds and results it reaches.

On the Person page, open Personnel assurance and choose Add record for assurance records: screening, training, policy acknowledgement and the like. Each record carries a category, requirement, status, completion and expiry dates, an authoritative source, an evidence link and notes. Review records as they near expiry; the screening or training itself happens through the responsible team or provider.

Connect suppliers to assets

In Vendors, record the supplier's name, description, website, contact and the assets it supplies. An asset has one supplying vendor, so assigning it to a new vendor moves the relationship; check the current supplier before saving. Supplier risks and evidence belong in the risk and evidence records.

Maintain the asset matrix

In ISMS → Asset matrix, enter the asset, its category and subcategory, a licence where applicable, the owner, the Register held by relationship, a description and the relevant controls and clauses. Category, subcategory and licence lists have their own forms. Ownership and organisational responsibility are different from who is currently using a device.

The asset detail page shows its relationships and changelog, and Sample into audit where available. When equipment moves or a service retires, update the asset's risks, supplier and people together.

Record access responsibilities

The Access control matrix records a task or action, the service, managed hosting accounts, status, who approved it, the roles and notes. Add a hosting-account record first if needed, link the intended roles and note the approval basis. Compare the matrix with real upstream permissions during access reviews.

Grant and revoke access in the external service itself. The matrix records the intended access. Keep passwords and API keys in your secret store and out of these records. Application Configuration records document context in the same spirit.

Maintain a connected asset register

The asset matrix and the assets used by risk assessments are the same records. Create an asset once and select it in risks, supplier records and personnel relationships. An asset can be a device, an information set, a service or an agreed group; say which in its description.

  1. Keep the organisation chart current first: an asset's owner and register custodian are links to its nodes.
  2. In ISMS → Asset matrix, create or edit the asset's name and description, choose a category and a subcategory that belongs to it, and a licence where applicable.
  3. Set Owner (who is responsible for the asset) and Register held by (who maintains its inventory information).
  4. Link controls and clauses in the selected framework, save, and check the links and changelog on the detail page.
  5. In People and Vendors, associate the asset with its people and supplier. In Risks, select the existing asset for the assessments that concern it.
  6. When the asset changes, review its risks, users, supplier and controls together. A name change never needs a duplicate asset.

Example. Create “Staff laptops” with a description of which devices it covers, an IT ownership node and a register custodian. Link the endpoint controls, the supplier and the people, and use the asset in a loss or theft risk. In an audit, sample the asset together with real endpoint evidence; the register entry alone only shows what was recorded.

Person and Vendor pages

The register contains searchable People and Vendor tables. Each name opens a dedicated page. Use Edit person or Edit vendor to change details; saving returns to the corresponding register tab. The Assets tab shows linked assets, and Related items shows available SWOT items and Stories. Vendor pages keep supplier details and supplied assets together.

A Person's Related items also shows current quiz assignments and round history, including not-started, in-progress, awaiting-grading and completed attempts. Named results require quiz.manage alongside People read access; ordinary quiz readers continue to see cohort results in Quizzes. No questions or answers appear in this summary. Quiz completion remains separate from manually maintained personnel-assurance records.

Workstation posture

Open a Person from the register and select Workstation posture to see their registered workstations. Each device shows a completion count out of five: disk encryption, automatic screen locking, an antivirus component, firewall and automatic update installation. Evidence read access is required alongside access to the People register.

Each result links to its retained evidence, provider, scope and observation time. Met means the current reported values meet the displayed target. Antivirus component detection counts towards that target; an explicit disabled protection state needs attention. Not established, Stale and Managed externally do not count as met. Qubes outer-layer controls are managed externally.

Reports are current for 24 hours from their observation time. Delayed or repeated delivery does not refresh that time. Revoked or expired credentials stop contributing completed checks. Refresh the panel for current results. Devices are paginated in groups of 20; the combined counter covers the displayed page.

Results describe reported scopes and settings. Disk and screen-lock completion requires all indexed scopes to meet the target. For antivirus, firewall and updates, a current positive provider can establish the target when no current scope explicitly reports failure. Update configuration does not prove every package was installed. Review the evidence for exclusions, conflicts and scope. Retention removes a result when its evidence is deleted.

Account provisioning creates the Person linked to the user. People without accounts remain supported. To transfer a device, ask an administrator to change its agent assignment; the new overview starts with subsequent reports.
