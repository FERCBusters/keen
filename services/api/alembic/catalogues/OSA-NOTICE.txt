Keen includes an offline OSA catalogue snapshot dated 12 November 2026
(osa-data commit 24214adf59806a060ddcef6582089283e5183d42).
Individual records retain their upstream framework URL, licence and source
commit. Keen adaptations include database identifiers, hierarchy, shortened
display titles and relationships derived from shared NIST control references.
Keen shows OSA's mapping note, gaps and coverage estimate for a control as a
crosswalk to NIST SP 800-53, apart from the control's description. Original
source fields remain in record metadata.
