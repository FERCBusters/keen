## HIPAA

`hipaa-2026.json` contains US federal regulatory text from 45 CFR Part 164,
Subparts C, D and E (Security, Breach Notification and Privacy), as published by
the eCFR at 6 October 2026. US federal government regulatory text is public domain.
https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-10-06/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164

The date is a snapshot date, not an edition of HIPAA. No proposed amendments have
been incorporated. Section and paragraph identifiers are taken directly from
eCFR HTML. Titles use paragraph headings or shortened opening excerpts; full
paragraph text is retained in descriptions, including child provisions. Grouping,
definition, applicability and transition entries initially sit outside scope;
leaf provisions initially sit in scope and require an applicability review.

These catalogues cover Subparts C/D/E only. Part 164 Subpart A, Parts 160/162,
other laws, agency guidance and court decisions must be considered separately.
The eCFR is an authoritative but unofficial compilation; consult current law and
HHS guidance. A control mapping or completed audit in KEEN is not a determination
of HIPAA compliance. Addressable security specifications require assessment and
documentation under 164.306(d)(3); they are not simply optional.
